# Cascade Retail Annual Report 2022

Fiscal Year 2022 - Cascade Retail (CSCD) - Sector: Consumer Retail

Fictional multi-format retailer operating across the United Kingdom. All companies, people and figures in this document are fictional and were generated for software demonstration.

All amounts are expressed in GBP millions unless otherwise stated. The fiscal year ended 31 December 2022.

## Management's Discussion and Analysis

Turnover increased 7.3% to £6,007.3 million in fiscal 2022, from £5,600.0 million in 2021.

Operating income margin was 17.6% for fiscal 2022, reflecting cost discipline across the group.

Segment turnover from Division A was £1,552.4 million and Division B contributed £2,147.5 million in fiscal 2022.

In fiscal 2021, Turnover was £5,600.0 million, which provides the comparison base for this year.

Management expects Turnover of £6,247.6 million in fiscal 2023; this guidance is a forward-looking statement and not a reported result.

Adjusted EBITDA was £1,605.7 million in fiscal 2022; adjusted measures exclude restructuring and one-off items and are not comparable with reported EBITDA.

Green capital expenditure was not separately disclosed for fiscal 2022.
