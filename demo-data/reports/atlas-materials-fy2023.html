# Atlas Materials Annual Report 2023

Fiscal Year 2023 - Atlas Materials (ATLS) - Sector: Basic Materials

Fictional producer of cement, aggregates and specialty chemicals. All companies, people and figures in this document are fictional and were generated for software demonstration.

All amounts are expressed in USD billions unless otherwise stated. The fiscal year ended 31 December 2023.

## Management's Discussion and Analysis

Operating revenue increased 4.5% to $5.399 billion in fiscal 2023, from $5.165 billion in 2022.

Operating income margin was 9.6% for fiscal 2023, reflecting cost discipline across the group.

Segment operating revenue from Division A was $1.873 billion and Division B contributed $2.069 billion in fiscal 2023.

In fiscal 2022, Operating revenue was $5.165 billion, which provides the comparison base for this year.

Management expects Operating revenue of $5.615 billion in fiscal 2024; this guidance is a forward-looking statement and not a reported result.

Adjusted EBITDA was $0.933 billion in fiscal 2023; adjusted measures exclude restructuring and one-off items and are not comparable with reported EBITDA.

Green capital expenditure was $0.069 billion in fiscal 2023, directed to low-carbon assets and efficiency projects.
