# Atlas Materials Annual Report 2022

Fiscal Year 2022 - Atlas Materials (ATLS) - Sector: Basic Materials

Fictional producer of cement, aggregates and specialty chemicals. All companies, people and figures in this document are fictional and were generated for software demonstration.

All amounts are expressed in USD billions unless otherwise stated. The fiscal year ended 31 December 2022.

## Management's Discussion and Analysis

Operating revenue increased 7.6% to $5.165 billion in fiscal 2022, from $4.800 billion in 2021.

Operating income margin was 9.6% for fiscal 2022, reflecting cost discipline across the group.

Segment operating revenue from Division A was $1.753 billion and Division B contributed $1.986 billion in fiscal 2022.

In fiscal 2021, Operating revenue was $4.800 billion, which provides the comparison base for this year.

Management expects Operating revenue of $5.372 billion in fiscal 2023; this guidance is a forward-looking statement and not a reported result.

Adjusted EBITDA was $0.926 billion in fiscal 2022; adjusted measures exclude restructuring and one-off items and are not comparable with reported EBITDA.

Green capital expenditure was $0.065 billion in fiscal 2022, directed to low-carbon assets and efficiency projects.
