# Meridian Industrial Annual Report 2022

Fiscal Year 2022 - Meridian Industrial (MRDN) - Sector: Industrial Manufacturing

Fictional European maker of precision industrial components. All companies, people and figures in this document are fictional and were generated for software demonstration.

All amounts are expressed in EUR thousands unless otherwise stated. The fiscal year ended 31 December 2022.

## Management's Discussion and Analysis

Net sales increased 2.8% to €3,187.4 million in fiscal 2022, from €3,100.0 million in 2021.

Operating income margin was 9.9% for fiscal 2022, reflecting cost discipline across the group.

Segment net sales from Division A was €734.5 million and Division B contributed €984.0 million in fiscal 2022.

In fiscal 2021, Net sales was €3,100.0 million, which provides the comparison base for this year.

Management expects Net sales of €3,314.9 million in fiscal 2023; this guidance is a forward-looking statement and not a reported result.

Adjusted EBITDA was €631.9 million in fiscal 2022; adjusted measures exclude restructuring and one-off items and are not comparable with reported EBITDA.

Green capital expenditure was €80.0 million in fiscal 2022, directed to low-carbon assets and efficiency projects.
