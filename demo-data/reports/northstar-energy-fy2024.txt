# Northstar Energy Annual Report 2024

Fiscal Year 2024 - Northstar Energy (NSTR) - Sector: Energy & Utilities

Fictional integrated power producer with gas, wind and solar generation. All companies, people and figures in this document are fictional and were generated for software demonstration.

All amounts are expressed in USD millions unless otherwise stated. The fiscal year ended 31 December 2024.

## Management's Discussion and Analysis

Revenue increased 4.7% to $9,967.0 million in fiscal 2024, from $9,520.8 million in 2023.

Operating income margin was 12.7% for fiscal 2024, reflecting cost discipline across the group.

Segment revenue from Division A was $3,282.7 million and Division B contributed $3,399.6 million in fiscal 2024.

In fiscal 2023, Revenue was $9,520.8 million, which provides the comparison base for this year.

Management expects Revenue of $10,365.7 million in fiscal 2025; this guidance is a forward-looking statement and not a reported result.

Adjusted EBITDA was $2,209.1 million in fiscal 2024; adjusted measures exclude restructuring and one-off items and are not comparable with reported EBITDA.

Green capital expenditure was $248.3 million in fiscal 2024, directed to low-carbon assets and efficiency projects.
